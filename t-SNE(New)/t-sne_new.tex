\title{Equilibrium Distribution for t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding with Generalized Kernels}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
    t-distributed stochastic neighbor embedding (t-SNE) is a well-known algorithm for visualizing high-dimensional data by finding low-dimensional representations (\cite{key}). In this paper, we study the convergence of t-SNE with generalized kernels and extend the results of \cite{auffinger-tsne}. Our work starts by giving a concrete formulation of generalized input and output kernels. Then we prove that under certain conditions, the t-SNE algorithm converges to an equilibrium distribution for a wide range of input and output kernels.
\end{abstract}
