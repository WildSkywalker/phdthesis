## Yi Gu's Ph.D Thesis
### Department of Mathematics, Northwestern University

This is the Ph.D thesis by Yi. Below is a quick rundown of the file organization.

The final version of the thesis is in the "Thesis Main" folder. It consists of 3 projects/problems I tackled during my Ph.D. The first problem is about the convergence and equilibrium measure of the t-SNE algorithm, with generalized input/output kernels. The paper itself can be found in the "t-SNE" folder. The second chapter is about the application of Lovász Local Lemma and a machine learning framework that can tackle #P problems such as k-CNF. One can find the related paper in the "AAAI Markov Random Field" folder. The third and final chapter is about Bayesian learning and variational auto encoders. The paper is located in the "EDM variational Auto Encoder" folder.

I have also included the my deprecated problem regarding spin glass and statistical physics in this repo. Though the problem has reached a dead end, or a stage that computation seems intangible, it is indeed a fascinating problem and worth documenting.
