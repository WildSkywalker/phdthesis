# Yi Gu's Ph.D Thesis
## Department of Mathematics, Northwestern University

### Overview
This is the Ph.D thesis by Yi. Below is a quick rundown of the file organization.

The final version of the [thesis](Thesis%20Main/phdthesis_YiGu.pdf) is in the "Thesis Main" folder. It consists of 3 projects/problems I tackled during my Ph.D. The first problem is about the convergence and equilibrium measure of the [t-SNE algorithm](t-SNE(New)/t-sne_new.pdf), with generalized input/output kernels. The paper itself can be found in the "t-SNE" folder. The second chapter is about the application of Lovász Local Lemma and a [machine learning framework](AAAI%20Markov%20Random%20Field/MRF.pdf) that can tackle #P problems such as k-CNF. One can find the related paper in the "AAAI Markov Random Field" folder. The third and final chapter is about Bayesian learning and [variational auto encoders](EDM%20Variational%20Auto%20Encoder/EDM_Article_Submission.pdf). The paper is located in the "EDM variational Auto Encoder" folder.

I have also included the my deprecated problem regarding spin glass and statistical physics in this repo. Though the problem has reached a stage that further computation seems intangible, it is indeed a fascinating problem and worth documenting.
